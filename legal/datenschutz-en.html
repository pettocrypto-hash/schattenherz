2. Data on your device

The app stores on your device: your save game (chosen name, progress and choices in the stories, gems, lanterns, outfits), your settings, your age confirmation and the receipts of your purchases (product ID and Google Play purchase token), so that no purchase is credited twice or not at all. In the Android app this data lives in the app's private storage, in the web version in your browser's storage.

We do not receive this data. It is not sent to us and not analysed. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the game) and Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (storage strictly necessary for the game you asked for).

Deleting: in the settings under “Delete save data”, or by uninstalling the app or clearing the site data in your browser. If you have turned on Android backup to your Google account, Android backs up only the save game, encrypted, to your Google account; you control this in the Android settings.

3. Android app: ads with Google AdMob

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

  • Optional only: an ad appears only when you tap an ad button yourself (chest or lantern), and it earns you a reward. There are no banners and no ads that open unasked. The app allows Google to show ads rated up to “Teen” (T) only.
  • Consent: after the age check, the app asks Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform, based on the IAB TCF standard) on every start whether you need to make a choice – in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the first time and whenever your choice expires. There you decide whether Google may use data for personalised ads and store information on your device. If you decline, Google shows limited, non-personalised ads at most.
  • Which data: when loading and showing an ad, the Google ads SDK processes in particular your IP address (and from it your approximate location), your device's advertising ID and app set ID, device and app information (model, Android version, language), interactions with the ad (views, taps) and diagnostic data. Purposes: delivering and measuring ads, limiting frequency, preventing fraud and abuse and – only with consent – personalisation.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG); for fraud prevention and security our legitimate interest in safe ad delivery (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Withdrawing or changing: at any time in the app's settings under “Privacy options” (shown where Google requires it). You can reset or delete the advertising ID in the Android settings.
  • USA: Google may transfer data to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission).

More: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The web version contains no real ads and loads no ads SDK.

4. Android app: purchases through Google Play

Gems, the starter pack, the season pass and the “Schattenherz Plus” subscription are bought only through Google Play Billing (Google Ireland Limited). You enter payment details only with Google; we receive no payment data. From Google Play the app only receives the product ID, the purchase token and the purchase state, to credit, confirm and later restore the purchase; this stays on your device (section 2). There is no server of our own that checks purchases. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

In the Google Play Console we only see what Google provides to process purchases (for example order number, product, country and amount), plus aggregated statistics on installs and crashes. You manage and cancel subscriptions in Google Play under “Payments & subscriptions”. The privacy policy and terms of Google Play also apply.

6. What there is not

No user account, no analytics or statistics services, no cookies of our own, no sharing or selling of your data, no chats and no data you send to other players.

7. Age limit

Schattenherz is for adults aged 18 and over. The app asks for your age before you first play; ads start only after that.

You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example where you live. As we store no data about you ourselves, many requests concern Google; you are still welcome to write to [E-MAIL] and we will help.
